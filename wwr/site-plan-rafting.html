Overview

Purpose

Wild Rivers is a white-water rafting company that offers both beginner and extreme rafting trips. The website will help visitors learn about the different trips, prices, discounts, and how to book an adventure.

Audience

The main audience is families, couples, and solo travelers who want to try white-water rafting. Both beginners and experienced people are welcome. Couple and family discounts will also be available here.
